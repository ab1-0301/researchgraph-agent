❓ How it works

What is ResearchGraph Agent?

It helps international students and researchers turn dense academic materials into concept maps, learning gaps, seminar questions, and presentation-ready action plans.

Workflow

  1. Ingest — Upload or paste papers, slides, or notes.
  2. Reason — Agent extracts concepts, relationships, and gaps.
  3. Act — View knowledge graph, report, and action plan.
  4. Study — Review concept cards, relationship chains, and self-quizzes.
  5. Review — Anki-style flashcards with SM-2 spaced repetition. Low-rated cards trigger deep-dive analysis.

Graph Controls

  • • Drag nodes to move them
  • • Scroll to zoom
  • • Click a node for details
  • • Click an edge for relationship info
